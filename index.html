Common questions

What is Wazuh, and is it really a SIEM?

Wazuh is an open-source security platform that combines SIEM and XDR capabilities. It collects and analyses logs, detects threats, monitors file integrity, finds vulnerabilities and can trigger response actions, all from one dashboard.

Is Wazuh really free?

The software itself has no licence fee. The costs are the infrastructure to run it and the expertise to deploy, tune and operate it well. That is where we help, and we confirm the scope and costs in a written proposal.

Where can Wazuh be hosted?

On your own servers, in Microsoft Azure or in AWS. We support both clouds, and we recommend the option that fits your size, budget and data requirements.

What can it monitor?

Windows, Linux and macOS systems through agents, firewalls and network devices through their logs, and cloud services such as Microsoft 365, Azure and AWS. We confirm the exact list with you before starting.

Is Wazuh the right choice compared with a paid SIEM?

It depends on your team and needs. Wazuh suits organisations that want control and lower software cost, but it needs skilled setup and tuning to work well. We will tell you honestly if a different approach fits you better.

Do you manage it after deployment?

Yes, if you want that. We can provide ongoing management, alert tuning, monitoring support and reporting. You can also take over the platform yourselves after hand-over. Scope is agreed per engagement.

Will Wazuh make us ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR or HIPAA compliant?

No. Wazuh gives you monitoring and evidence that support your readiness and audits. It is not a certification and does not guarantee compliance.

How long does deployment take?

It depends on the number of systems and log sources. We give you a timeline once we understand the scope.
